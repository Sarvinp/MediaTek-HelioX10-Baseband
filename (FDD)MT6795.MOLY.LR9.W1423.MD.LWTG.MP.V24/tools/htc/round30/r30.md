## Interpretation

(the probe proved this executes every boot). Yet the RIL doesn't
advance. Two candidate reasons:
1. +EIND:2 IS emitted at modem boot but BEFORE the RIL (re)attaches;

   exchange (R26: l4csmu_security_cnf_hdlr silent).

   print (fix5 nopped the begin-side bit only... actually fix5 nopped
   finish gate 0x3a1fb6 — that patch is NOT in fix6clean).
