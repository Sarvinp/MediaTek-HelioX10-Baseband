## Interpretation (best current theory)
The modem-side chain completes and URCs reach the AP. The blocker is
the LAST MILE: MTK RIL on silent-reboot path requires the modem's
eboot/version handshake (`gsm.ril.eboot` stays -1 = RIL never got the
eboot event it needs to re-run SIM init flow). Our V24-era AT stack
doesn't send the newer boot handshake sequence (stock's 2508-era
build does; its early-boot URC set differs — stock sends a richer
EIND/EBOOT family we couldn't capture before buffer rotation).

Also missing on our side (decoded from string table):

  family; ours only 64/128.
